title: "Download Devotionals, Summaries & Clips"
---

If devotional content or a summary is generated from your video, you can download it in:

- PDF
- DOCX

You can review and edit the content before downloading.

## How to Download a Summary

1. Go to the **Clip listing page**.
2. Click the **ellipsis (⋯) icon** for the clip.
3. Choose the format you want to download.

Summaries are available in:

- PDF
- DOCX

---

## How to Download a Devotional

The process is the same:

1. Go to the **Clip listing page**.
2. Click the **ellipsis (⋯) icon** on the devotional item.
3. Select your preferred download format.

---

## How to Download a Clip

To download a processed clip:

1. Go to the Clip listing page.
2. Click the **Download button** on the clip you want.
3. The clip will begin downloading immediately.
