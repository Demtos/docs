title: "How Do Credits Work?"
---

## What Are Credits?

Credits are used to process videos on Demtos Clip.

Each time you submit a video for clipping, credits are deducted based on the length of the video being processed.

Currently:

- **1 credit = 1 minute of video processed**

For example:

- A 30-minute video uses 30 credits
- A 10-minute selected timeframe uses 10 credits

---

## How Is Credit Usage Calculated?

Credit usage is based on the length of the video (or selected timeframe).

When submitting a video, you can:

- Process the **entire video**, or
- Select a **specific timeframe**

Credits are calculated based on whole minutes:

- Videos shorter than 1 minute are rounded up to 1 credit
- Partial minutes are rounded down to the nearest whole minute

---

## How Many Credits Are Included in My Plan?

Each plan includes a set number of credits per billing cycle.

You can view your available credits from your account dashboard at any time.

---

## Do Credits Expire?

Credits are valid for the duration of your billing cycle.

If your subscription renews, your credits reset according to your plan.

(If you plan to allow rollover, we can adjust this wording.)

---

## How Do I Get More Credits?

If you run out of credits, you can:

- Upgrade your plan
- Purchase additional credits (if available under your plan)

Changes take effect immediately after payment is confirmed.
