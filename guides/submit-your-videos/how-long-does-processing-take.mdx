---
title: "How Long Does Processing Take?"
---

Processing time depends on the length of your video.

In general:

- Short videos process faster
- Longer videos take more time
- Server traffic may also affect processing speed

For example, a 10-minute video will process much faster than a 60-minute sermon.

Because Demtos Clip analyzes your full content before generating clips, longer videos naturally require more processing time.

---

## Can I close the page while it’s processing?

Yes. You do **not** need to keep the page open.

Once you submit your video:

- Processing continues in the background
- You can close the tab or leave the dashboard
- Your project will remain saved in your account

---

## Will I be notified when processing is complete?

Yes.

When your video finishes processing:

- You will receive an **email notification**
- Your clips will be available in your dashboard
- You can review, edit, and download them at any time

If you don’t receive the notification email, please check your Spam or Junk folder.
