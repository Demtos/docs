---
title: "I Didn’t Receive My Verification Email"
---

If you signed up for Demtos Clip but did not receive your verification email, don’t worry — this is usually easy to fix.

## 1. Check Your Spam or Junk Folder

Verification emails are sometimes filtered automatically.\
Please check:

- Spam
- Junk
- Promotions (Gmail)
- Updates tab (Gmail)

Search your inbox for: **Demtos**

---

## 2. Wait a Few Minutes

Email delivery can sometimes take a few minutes depending on your provider.

If you just signed up, wait 2–5 minutes and refresh your inbox.

---

## 3. Make Sure Your Email Was Entered Correctly

A small typo (for example, [gmial.com](http://gmial.com) instead of [gmail.com](http://gmail.com)) will prevent the email from arriving.

If you suspect a typo, try signing up again with the correct email address.

---

## 4. Resend the Verification Code

If the option is available, click **Resend Verification Email** on the login page.

---

## 5. Use Google Login Instead

If you're still having issues, you can sign up or log in using **Google** instead of email.

---

## 6. Still Not Working?

If none of the above works, contact our support team and include:

- The email address you used
- The time you attempted to sign up

We’ll help you resolve it quickly.
