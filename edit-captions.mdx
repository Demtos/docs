## Edit Captions

You can edit captions directly inside the editor before exporting your clip.

This allows you to adjust wording, fix formatting, and make corrections before final download.

## How to Edit Captions

You can edit captions directly inside the Editor.

1. Open your project.
2. Go to the **Transcript panel** inside the Editor.
3. Click on any text you want to edit.
4. Make your changes directly in the text field.
5. Your changes will automatically update in the clip.

This allows you to adjust wording, fix formatting, and correct any transcription errors before exporting your clip.
