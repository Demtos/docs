---
title: "Plans & Pricing"
---

Demtos Clip offers three simple plans based on how many credits you need.

Credits are used to process your videos.\
**1 credit = 1 minute of video processing.**

---

## Free Plan (One-Time)

- 120 credits included
- One-time allocation (does not renew)
- No watermark on processed videos

The Free plan is designed to let you try Demtos Clip and experience the full processing workflow.

Once your 120 credits are used, you’ll need to upgrade to continue processing videos.

---

## Starter Plan

- 300 credits per month
- Available with monthly or yearly billing
- Credits renew every billing cycle

---

## Pro Plan

- 600 credits per month
- Available with monthly or yearly billing
- Credits renew every billing cycle

---

## Do you offer yearly plans?

Yes. Starter and Pro plans are available with both monthly and yearly billing options.

Yearly subscriptions are billed annually and include a **17% discount** compared to monthly billing.

Credits are allocated monthly, even on yearly plans.

All features remain the same — yearly billing simply offers savings for long-term users.

---

## Do credits expire?

- Free plan credits do not renew.
- Starter and Pro credits reset every billing cycle.
- Unused credits do not roll over to the next billing cycle.

---

## What if I run out of credits before the month ends?

If you exhaust your credits before your billing cycle renews, you can purchase additional credits without upgrading your plan.

This allows you to continue processing videos immediately.

---

## Can I buy extra credits?

Yes. Paid users can purchase additional credits at any time if they need additional processing capacity before their next billing cycle.
