- PDF

This makes it easy to reuse your transcript for captions, publishing, or archiving.

## How to Download a Transcript

You can download your transcript from the Project card.

1. Go to your Projects page.
2. Locate the project you want.
3. Click the **ellipsis (⋯) icon** on the project card.
4. Select your preferred format.

Available formats:

- PDF
- DOCX
- SRT
- TXT

The transcript will download in the format you select.
